      About LakeHouse

      LakeHouse is a rock group from Hamilton, NJ with a big sound and a small footprint — at home filling a large venue or playing an intimate event. Ron Heller and Jeff Mason cover a wide range of styles, from Sinatra to Avicii and Hall & Oates to Matchbox Twenty.

      The idea started when Jeff asked Ron to play at senior citizen homes as a way to give back to the community. It grew from there.

      Ron Heller

      Vocals · Bass guitar · Saxophone

      Ron is the bandleader and producer of LakeHouse and runs live sound and lights. A University of the Arts graduate in saxophone and music education, he has performed with the Glenn Miller Orchestra, New York Voices, Grover Washington Jr., Arturo Sandoval and Jimmy Bruno, and has led his own jazz quintet.

      He's played with Philadelphia-area wedding and cover bands for over 25 years, is the saxophonist for Exit28, and leads Your Love Is King — a Tribute to Sade.

      Jeff Mason

      Vocals

      Jeff hails from Hamilton, NJ and is known for his wide range and his ability to connect with an audience. He was the lead singer of Cheap Date, a central New Jersey rock band.
